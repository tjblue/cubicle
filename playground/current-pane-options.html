Sample content

Select a pane to focus it. Try split right/down, fold, zoom, close, drag dividers, and double-click a header to fold. The + button adds a chooser.

What the current design already does

Compact and content-first: 32 px headers and actions revealed on hover or focus. Focus: a restrained gold border and glow. Pane actions: split right, split down, fold, zoom, and close; double-click folds. States: a gold attention dot and a persistent queue badge. The chooser offers Terminal, File, and Browser. This mockup reproduces those visual cues and simulates the interactions.
