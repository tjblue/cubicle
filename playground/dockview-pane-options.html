What I found in Cubicle

Group-first layout: Dockview’s tab strip has a plus button for a tab in the same group. Split controls: two buttons occupy a separate toolbar under each tab strip. New tab: the address bar opens a browser, and cards open Files or Terminal. Visual language: flat, square-edged groups with blue-gray surfaces and compact controls. The presets above change those details while keeping that structure recognizable.
